## Authored business puzzles

Case-032 (free coffee) and case-044 (repeat purchases) use entirely authored businesses, quantities, cash timing, research findings and events. They are not historical claims, recommendations for a particular business, or verified results of the transcribed advertisements. Each calculation describes only the stated scope.

Coffee arithmetic: normal sales 100×4 + 20×8 = 560; costs 100×1 + 20×3 + 250 = 410; remainder 150. Free-day forecast: 300×20% = 60 breakfasts; sales 480; costs 300 + 180 + 250 = 730; loss 250. Sponsor adds 200, leaving a 50 loss. Thirty new prepaid bundles add 270 sales and 120 costs to normal trading, leaving 300 total. Without sponsorship, 140 breakfasts match normal profit but exceed the 100-breakfast capacity. With sponsorship, 100 breakfasts match normal profit.

Acquisition arithmetic per 100 buyers: first sales 4,000; fulfilment 3,500; ads 2,000; loss 1,500. A completed group's 150 refills add 4,500 sales and 1,500 costs, adding 3,000 contribution. Total 90-day contribution 1,500. New ad quote adds 500 cost, reducing prospective contribution to 1,000 if repeat demand holds. It also creates a 500 upfront funding shortfall. Paid starter sales then release 500, so deferring at least 500 of the ad bill until their settlement solves the stated timing gap if the platform agrees. No agreement is presumed.

## Historical case-009: Amazon Prime

Decision cutoff: launch review in February 2005. The historical proposal is reconstructed from the contemporaneous announcement; these are not alleged transcripts of an internal Amazon meeting. Existing research provides launch terms and company cash figures. Missing member economics is a limitation of supplied sources, not evidence that Amazon lacked forecasts.

- [Amazon's February 2, 2005 launch announcement and 2004 results](https://press.aboutamazon.com/2005/2/amazon-com-announces-record-free-cash-flow-fueled-by-lower-prices-and-free-shipping-introduces-new-express-shipping-program-amazon-prime): confirms $79 annual fee, unlimited two-day shipping without a purchase minimum, $3.99 next-day upgrade, sharing with up to four household family members, $477m 2004 free cash flow versus $346m in 2003, and $6.92bn 2004 sales. Opened and verified live. Proposal and financial facts only before commitment; launch outcome in reveal.
- [Amazon's 2017 shareholder letter](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/company-news/2017-letter-to-shareholders): confirms more than 100m paid Prime members globally thirteen years after launch. Opened and verified live. Reveal only. This establishes adoption, not profit attributable to the original plan or proof the player's alternative would fail.

The halfway shipping-team request, options, tradeoffs and scores are authored teaching material. Both a full launch and a representative test receive strong initial scores; agreement with Amazon's historical decision is not the grading rule. No Amazon member-profit numbers are invented.
