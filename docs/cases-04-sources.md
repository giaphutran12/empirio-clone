# Cases 04–12: source and timeline audit

Audited 2026-09-20. Each linked primary source was opened and inspected using the web tool. Search snippets were discovery only. The failed Microsoft developer-blog fetch was not used; official April and November newsroom releases were opened instead.

These are authored decision exercises grounded in historical inputs. A release-day launch brief reconstructs proposed terms immediately before public announcement; it is not a claim that an archived internal meeting contained the exact options. Forecasts stay forecasts. Unknown means the case lacks that input, not that the real executives lacked it. No future outcome is in player evidence.

Every three-hour event is authored scenario pressure, tagged `simulation`, with no source IDs. Options, interpretations, conditional consequences and rubric questions are authored judgment aids, not reported historical facts. Research tasks cost 1, 2, 2 and 3 hours. Each has at least two source-backed factual unlocks across the task set. Company and product aliases plus source URLs exist only under `reveal`.
