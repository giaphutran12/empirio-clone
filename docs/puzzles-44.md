# Business puzzles 44–50: authored inputs and arithmetic audit

These seven cases use authored business inputs, not historical company accounts. No external business claims or invented sources are needed. All prices are US dollars. Initial evidence and paid research have `simulation` provenance. Research results remain separate from opening facts. Opening grades use only opening evidence. Event grades use the openly delivered update. Each case has research costs 2, 2 and 3 hours, so all research cannot fit into six hours.

## 44 · Ad sales versus money kept

- Opening 100 orders: sales 100 × $40 = $4,000. Goods $2,500 + delivery/fees $800 + ads $1,000 = $4,300. Contribution is minus $300, before rent and salaries.
- Doubling at unchanged efficiency: $8,000 sales minus $8,600 costs = minus $600. This is a conditional example, not promised demand.
- $45 price with unchanged volume: $4,500 minus $4,300 = $200 before overhead. Buyer response is unknown.
- Paid quote reduces goods plus delivery/fees to $31, leaving $9 before $10 ads, still minus $1. Research repeat orders add 10 × ($40 − $33) = $70, which does not erase $300.
- Visible event changes ad cost to $12 per order: $40 − $33 − $12 = minus $5. No exact order count claimed for a $2,000 budget at this rate.
- Practice: $40 − $31 − $10 = minus $1.

## 45 · Profitable order, unfunded bill

- 3,000 × $10 = $30,000 sales. Goods 3,000 × $6 = $18,000; delivery $2,000. Contribution $10,000 if collected, before overhead.
- Cash $12,000 less $4,000 reserve = $8,000 spendable. $20,000 upfront costs leave a $12,000 funding gap without a deposit.
- Proposed 50% deposit = $15,000. $15,000 + $8,000 = $23,000, enough to cover the order while protecting the reserve. Grade credits insisting on payment, not assuming buyer consent.
- Visible event caps deposit at 20% = $6,000. Total usable funds $14,000, funding gap $6,000. The changed option refuses commitment until funding exists.
- Smaller order has no invented delivery quote and is excluded from the table. Deposit is part of sales, never counted as extra revenue.
- Practice: unpaid balance $30,000 − $15,000 = $15,000.

## 46 · Discount switching

- Full-price contribution $12 − $4 = $8 per guest. Discount contribution $6 − $4 = $2.
- 40 discounted visits contribute $80. If 10 replace regular visits, forgone contribution is 10 × $8 = $80. Net added contribution zero.
- Five switchers instead: $80 − 5 × $8 = $40 incremental contribution (practice answer).
- Table compares the deal with counterfactual regular visits explicitly. It is not a profit table: unchanged rent/staff and unmodeled snack sales are excluded.
- Paid survey states intentions, not observed causality. Historical-account targeting reduces switching risk but does not prove zero switching.
- Visible event introduces repeated broad-coupon use. No unsupported dollar forecast for later uses is asserted. Only the broad-coupon option changes.

## 47 · Stock is not cash

- At 100 sales monthly, three months bring 300 × $20 = $6,000 sales.
- Monthly ordering: cost of goods sold 300 × $10 = $3,000; contribution $3,000 before shop bills.
- Bulk ordering: 500 × $8 = $4,000 cash spent; cost of 300 sold units $2,400, contribution $3,600. Remaining inventory 200 × $8 = $1,600. These are not $2,400 cash outflows.
- Opening $5,000 cash minus pallet $4,000 leaves $1,000 against $2,000 immediate bills, a $1,000 gap.
- Visible $500 repair raises immediate bills to $2,500; gap becomes $1,500. Table remains explicitly opening conditions.
- Split research offer commits two 250-unit batches, not free option rights. First payment $2,000, second $2,000. No claim that future stock demand is assured.
- Practice: remaining inventory cost $1,600, not cash.

## 48 · Fixed fee and owner time

- $1,200 / 20 hours = $60 hourly before edits; / 24 = $50; / 30 = $40; / 40 = $30.
- Thirty hourly hours at $50 earn $1,500. Scoped flat-fee illustration assumes 24 total hours, explicitly conditional. Unlimited flat-fee illustration assumes 30 total hours, not a ceiling.
- No cash costs are supplied; table therefore labels values fees before overhead and owner labor, not profit. Owner time remains economically relevant.
- Visible scope change raises initial draft estimate to 30 hours before edits; $1,200 / 30 = $40. Open-ended scope is worse even without using locked edit-history data.
- Practice: $1,200 / 40 = $30 hourly.

## 49 · Presales and break-even

- Each $50 ticket leaves $30 after $20 guest costs. Opening fixed bill $2,400 requires 80 guests.
- 60 guests: $3,000 sales − ($2,400 + $1,200) = minus $600.
- 80 guests: $4,000 sales − ($2,400 + $1,600) = zero.
- Paid smaller-package quote: $1,800 fixed, 70 capacity. At 60 guests, $3,000 − ($1,800 + $1,200) = zero.
- Visible update changes only original package to $2,700. Break-even becomes 90 × $30. Smaller quote explicitly unchanged. Table states opening costs; its 80-person row is not offered as the new break-even.
- Refundable presales are not safe-to-spend cash before confirmation. Canceling returns all $3,000 and incurs no bills under the stated assumptions.
- Practice: $2,700 / ($50 − $20) = 90 guests.

## 50 · Signup totals versus retained users

- Opening month: 500 + 100 − 90 = 510 users at month end. Starts/exits occur at month end to avoid unsupported prorated billing.
- Next-decision comparisons are the first full month after assumed changes, all starting at 510. Not predicted outcomes.
- Hold: no joins, 90 exits, 420 active. $8,400 sales − $2,100 service = $6,300 contribution before overhead.
- Repeat opening ads: 100 joins, 90 exits, 520 active. $10,400 − ($2,600 service + $2,000 marketing) = $5,800.
- Repair illustration: no joins, 60 exits (30 hypothetically saved), 450 active. $9,000 − ($2,250 service + $2,000 repair) = $4,750. Does not claim repair has first-month payback.
- Double opening ads: 200 joins, 90 exits, 620 active. $12,400 − ($3,100 service + $4,000 marketing) = $5,300 before overhead. Debt principal/interest not modeled; table explicitly is not a financing cash forecast.
- At $15 monthly user contribution, a $20 acquisition cost needs more than 1.33 paid months before overhead. A $2,000 repair needs more than 133 saved user-months. Unknown retention means neither investment's lifetime profit is established.
- Visible ad quote $25: $2,000 / $25 = 80 signups; repeating 90 exits means 510 + 80 − 90 = 500 users (practice answer).
