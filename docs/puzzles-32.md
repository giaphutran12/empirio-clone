# Business puzzles 32–37: arithmetic and evidence audit

All six are authored fictional scenarios. Dollar amounts are USD. They have no claimed historical results or external sources. Initial evidence contains the numbers used by baseline scores. Paid findings remain inside research; event findings remain inside the event and private debrief.

| Case | Opening comparison | Check and boundaries |
| --- | --- | --- |

Every case has three paid investigations at 2, 2 and 3 hours. Events affect one visible choice and its score. Forecasts, survey interest, and trials are explicitly distinguished from completed sales. Tables include all listed costs within their stated scope; the agency table clearly excludes separately covered fixed business costs and is called project surplus. No success outcome or causal claim is invented.
